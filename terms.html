1. Description of service

The System is a private operational monitoring tool. When configured, it sends SMS alert notifications to the system owner regarding events such as market data streaming failures, process crashes, authentication failures, and recovery failures.

2. Eligibility and intended recipient

SMS alerts are intended solely for the individual who deploys, configures, and operates the System on their own private server. 3. Consent

By entering a mobile phone number into the sms_alerts section of the System's config.yaml file and enabling the SMS feature, the operator provides explicit consent to receive SMS alert messages from the System.

4. Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on system events. Typically, the System sends between 0 and 10 messages per day. Frequency may be higher during incidents or extended outages.

6. Opt-out

The recipient may opt out of SMS alerts at any time by replying STOP to any message received from the System. The recipient may also opt out by removing the phone number from config.yaml, or by setting the enabled flag to false, and restarting the System.
