Privacy Policy — Project 5 SMS Alerts

Last updated: May 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Project 5 system ("the System") handles personal information in connection with SMS alert notifications sent to the system owner.

1. Scope

The System is a private operational monitoring tool used by its owner to receive SMS alerts about market data streaming failures, process crashes, authentication failures, and recovery failures. SMS alerts are sent only to the individual who deploys, configures, and operates the System on their own private server. The System does not send messages to the general public, customers, or third parties.

2. Information collected

The only personal information collected for SMS purposes is the mobile phone number that the system owner voluntarily enters into the sms_alerts section of the System's config.yaml configuration file. No other personal information is collected, transmitted, or stored in connection with the SMS alerting functionality.

3. How the information is used

The phone number is used solely to deliver SMS alert notifications generated by the System's monitoring logic. It is transmitted to the messaging service provider (Twilio) for the sole purpose of routing those alert messages.

Mobile information is not shared with third parties. Mobile phone numbers and any associated consent records are not sold, rented, leased, or shared with any third parties for marketing, promotional, or any other purposes. The only disclosure of the phone number is to the messaging service provider (Twilio) strictly for the technical purpose of delivering the SMS alerts that the owner has configured. No mobile information of any kind is shared with affiliates, partners, advertisers, or any other parties.

4. Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on system events. In normal operation, the System typically sends between 0 and 10 messages per day. During incidents or extended outages, frequency may be higher.

5. Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply. The recipient is responsible for any charges imposed by their mobile carrier in connection with receiving SMS messages.

6. Opt-out

The recipient may opt out of SMS alerts at any time by replying STOP to any message received from the System. Opt-out is also possible by removing the phone number from config.yaml or by setting the enabled flag to false and restarting the System.

7. Help

For assistance, the recipient may reply HELP to any message received from the System.

8. Data retention

The phone number is stored locally in the System's configuration file on the owner's private server. It is retained for as long as the owner chooses to keep it in the file. Removing the entry from config.yaml deletes the number from the System.

9. Security

The configuration file resides on a private server controlled by the system owner. Access to the server, and therefore to the configuration file, is restricted to the owner.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
